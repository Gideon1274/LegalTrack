The Platform

What is PASTrack?

PASTrack is a unified digital ecosystem that bridges the gap between Municipal Assessor's Offices and the Provincial Capitol, creating a seamless flow of data and documentation.

Digital Coordination

Centralized platform for real-time synchronization of property records across the province.

Efficiency Focused

Optimized workflows to reduce processing time from weeks to just a few business days.

High Accountability

Complete audit trails for every transaction, ensuring transparency for every citizen.

The Services

The PASTrack Journey

Our 6-step high-efficiency process ensures that every property declaration is handled with utmost accuracy and speed.

Step 01

Intake

Initial document receiving and logging into the PASTrack ecosystem at the LGU level.

Step 02

Examiner Review

Comprehensive verification of documents and property details by assigned examiners.

Step 03

Tax Mapping

Precise plotting and field verification on digital and physical tax maps for spatial accuracy.

Step 04

For Approval

Final review and formal signature by the Provincial Assessor for legal validation.

Step 05

Numbering

Official assignment of the unique Tax Declaration number for government records.

Step 06

Ready for Release

Final dispatching of approved documents back to the owner or LGU representative.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my property request?
Simply enter your Tracking ID in the search bar at the top of this page. The system will instantly show you the current location of your documents.
Where can I find my Tracking ID?
Your Tracking ID is printed on the Acknowledgment Receipt provided to you by the Municipal Assessor's Office.
What should I do if my Tracking ID is not found?
Double-check the characters. If it's still not found, your Municipal Assessor may not have uploaded it yet. Please contact them for confirmation.
